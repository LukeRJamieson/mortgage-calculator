    Estimates only. Assumes the rate stays fixed for the whole term and interest is charged each repayment period on the loan balance minus the offset. Debt-free means the earlier of the loan reaching $0 or your offset holding enough to pay it out. Offset growth is entered per month and spread evenly across repayments. Your repayment is converted between frequencies at the same yearly total. End dates count from today. Australian lenders usually calculate interest daily and charge it monthly, so your statement can differ by a few dollars. Repayments are rounded up to the cent. Fees, rate changes and redraws are not included.
